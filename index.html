Winnipeg Building Permits

Interactive reports on City of Winnipeg new-construction permits. Prepared by Jason M. Schellenberg, P. App, AACI.

Non-Residential Permits

New-construction permits filed under the Non-Residential permit group — commercial, institutional, industrial. Map, filters, and CSV export.

Multi-Residential Permits

New-construction permits with more than two dwelling units — apartments, condominiums, multi-family. Map, filters, cluster/neighbourhood overlay, and CSV export.
